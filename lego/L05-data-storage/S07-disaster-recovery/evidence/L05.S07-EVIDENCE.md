2. **Replication Lag Monitoring**: Automatically flags replica health transitions (`Healthy` vs `Degraded`) when lag exceeds defined SLA thresholds.
3. **Atomic Safe Failover Promotion**: Orchestrates clean demotion of legacy primary instances to prevent split-brain hazards and ensures only ready standbys assume primary duties.
4. **Transport-Neutral Replication Contract**: Exposes `port.storage.dr.sync.v1` and `port.storage.dr.replicate.v1` for distributed storage orchestration.
5. **Fail-Closed Security Boundary**: Invocations omitting valid principal, tenant, or authority scope are rejected with `PortStatus::SecurityDenied`.
