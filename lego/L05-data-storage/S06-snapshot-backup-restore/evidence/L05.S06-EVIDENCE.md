## Invariants Verified & Enforced
1. **Cryptographic Checksum Verification**: Every backup snapshot computes and validates SHA-256 integrity checksums prior to registration and restore operations.
2. **Tenant Isolation Enforcement**: Cross-tenant snapshot restoration attempts are rejected immediately with `BackupError::TenantMismatch`, strictly preventing data leakage across organizational tenants.
3. **Deletion & State Transition Safety**: Deleted snapshots are immutable and rejected on subsequent restore attempts.
4. **Transport-Neutral Port Dispatch**: Provides public ports for snapshot creation, inspection, and environment restoration.
5. **Fail-Closed Security Boundary**: Invocations omitting valid principal, tenant, or authority scope are denied with `PortStatus::SecurityDenied`.
