## Invariants Verified & Enforced
1. **Idempotent Retention Enforcement**: Retention policies enforce TTL limits and capacity caps across entity domains (executions, binary data) without duplicate deletion hazards.
2. **Deterministic Tombstoning**: Deletion candidates are marked with durable tombstones before physical purging occurs, enabling safe point-in-time recovery and snapshot replication.
3. **Physical Compaction & Space Reclamation**: Physical compaction passes accurately calculate reclaimed bytes, purge tombstones, and support non-destructive dry-run evaluations.
4. **Authoritative State Isolation**: The `retention-policy-index` state domain is protected with thread-safe synchronization (`RwLock`) and strictly isolated from cross-sublego private imports.
5. **Fail-Closed Security Boundary**: Invocations omitting valid tenant, principal, or authority scopes are denied (`PortStatus::SecurityDenied`).
