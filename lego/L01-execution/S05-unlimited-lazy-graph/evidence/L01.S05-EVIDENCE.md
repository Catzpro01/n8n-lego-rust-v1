## Invariants Verified & Enforced
1. **Lazy Frontier Expansion**: Evaluates successor nodes incrementally as prerequisites complete, avoiding full in-memory materialisation of unbounded workflows.
2. **Cycle Detection**: Tracks active ancestor chains during frontier expansion and immediately halts with `LazyGraphError::CycleDetected` on circular dependencies.
3. **Memory Boundedness**: Restricts maximum concurrent active nodes in the frontier via strict budget caps (`FrontierCapacityExceeded`), preventing out-of-memory crashes on infinite fan-outs.
4. **Authoritative State Isolation**: State domain `lazy-graph-frontier` is thread-safe, isolated, and strictly guarded against cross-sublego private imports.
5. **Fail-Closed Security Boundary**: Invocations missing valid principal, tenant, or authority scope are rejected with `PortStatus::SecurityDenied`.
